Training Data Guidelines
Image Requirements:
  • High quality okra leaf images
  • Clear, well-lit photos
  • JPG, PNG, or JPEG format
  • At least 224x224 pixels
  • Maximum 10MB per file
Best Practices:
  • Upload at least 20 images per disease class
  • Include different lighting conditions
  • Show various disease stages
  • Avoid blurry or damaged images
  • Validate images before training
